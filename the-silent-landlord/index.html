The courtroom and the concrete

The DOJ won its search case.

Remedies took effect on 3 February 2026. Filed

The court refused to order a Chrome divestiture.

The fight now sits at the DC Circuit, over the payment ban and Google's own appeal. Filed

Regulators can reach the software distribution.

They have a much harder time reaching the subsea cables, the TPU fleet, the nuclear contracts and the equity web.

That is my reading, not a ruling.

But it's the reading the balance sheet supports.

Your Deal Already Started Without You

Before the meeting

01

Intelligence: knows the company before you do

Tools: Clay · ZoomInfo · Apollo.io · LinkedIn Sales Navigator

Scans news, financials, leadership changes, job postings and the competitive landscape.

Maps the whole buying committee.

Not just your usual contact. The CFO, the CTO and the business owners who actually decide.

Replaces: hours of manual research before every meeting.

Illustrative example: You've worked one contact for two years. The agent surfaces the budget holder in minutes.

02

Signal: detects who's ready to buy

Tools: 6sense · Bombora · ZoomInfo Intent · Salesforce Einstein

Watches buying signals across the territory.

Website visits. Content engagement. Hiring patterns.

Scores accounts by likelihood to buy and surfaces dormant ones about to wake up.

Replaces: gut feel about where to spend the week.

Illustrative example: A quiet account posts three cloud engineering roles. Its CEO talks about "AI transformation" on a podcast. You didn't notice. The agent did.

03

Outreach: gets you in the room

Tools: Outreach · Salesloft · Apollo · Amplemarket

Personalised sequences across email, LinkedIn and phone.

Timing tuned on engagement data.

Messages built from the intelligence layer.

Replaces: generic templates to the wrong person at the wrong time.

Illustrative example: Not 50 generic emails. One message to the CFO that references her own post on cost optimisation.

04

Prep: briefs you like a chief of staff

Tools: Gong · Salesforce Einstein · custom AI

Pulls intelligence, signals, past interactions and positioning into one brief.

Talking points. Likely objections. The angle that wins.

Replaces: walking in and hoping the customer tells you what they need.

Illustrative example: "They looked at alternatives last quarter. The CFO cares about total cost. Lead with outcomes, not specs. Expect pushback on change risk."

The meeting

The meeting

The salesperson — irreplaceable

Trust.

Judgment.

Reading the room.

Knowing when to push and when to shut up and listen.

Twenty years of pattern recognition.

The human conversation that turns a qualified opportunity into a signed deal.

Replaces: nothing.

Illustrative example: Body language shifts when timelines come up. You pause. You ask what's really worrying them. There's a board deadline nobody mentioned. That saves the deal.

Your Deal Already Started Without You

After the meeting

05

Pipeline: keeps the deal alive

Tools: Salesforce Einstein · Coffee · HubSpot AI

Updates the CRM after every interaction.

Sends the summary and next steps.

Flags when a deal goes quiet or a champion goes dark.

Replaces: half an hour of CRM admin after every meeting.

Illustrative example: Meeting ends at 3pm. By 3:05 the CRM is updated, the summary is sent and the next steps are booked. You did nothing.

06

Revenue: tells you what's real

Tools: Gong · Clari · Salesforce Forecasting

Analyses conversations.

Spots winning talk tracks.

Forecasts on evidence, not optimism.

Replaces: "I think it'll close" pipeline reviews.

Illustrative example: Your forecast says $2M. Call sentiment and stakeholder engagement say $1.4M. Believe the evidence.

07

Growth: finds the next deal inside the current one

Tools: Gainsight · Totango · ChurnZero

Watches usage, satisfaction and engagement.

Spots churn risk months before renewal.

Spots expansion early.

Feeds it all back to Intelligence.

The cycle restarts, smarter.

Replaces: finding out about churn at renewal time.

Illustrative example: Active users up 40% in a quarter. Two new teams onboarded. Expansion flagged. You walk in prepared.

↻ Growth → Intelligence. The cycle restarts.
